Powerful Features

Everything You Need To Manage Stock

1. Inventory Management

Complete lifecycle management for your products including SKUs, suppliers, categories, and unit pricing.

2. Search and Filtering

Instant database queries by product name or SKU. Dynamic category and stock-level filter combinations.

3. Stock Monitoring

Automated stock status flags (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock) based on custom minimum safety thresholds.

4. Visual Analytics

Interactive Chart.js visual graphs breaking down category distributions, total inventory valuation, and metrics.

5. Stock History Audit

Full stock change transaction log recording timestamped stock additions, removals, and manual edits.

6. Secure User Accounts

Session-based authentication with Werkzeug password hashing and strict data isolation per account.
